🚀 Pitch Deck Outline

Judge Presentation

1. Problem

Over 1.2M restaurants & retail stores still schedule shifts manually. Shift managers waste 20+ hours a month managing WhatsApp messages, causing payroll overtime leaks, understaffed shifts, and high employee turnover.

2. Solution: AutoRoster AI

An intelligent scheduling assistant that converts messy human messages into structured constraints, solves the weekly roster mathematically in milliseconds, and instantly handles emergency call-outs.

3. Why We Win: Truthful Constraint Solving

Other AI tools hallucinate schedules that break labor laws or double-book workers. AutoRoster uses strict CSP logic: if a schedule is mathematically impossible, it diagnoses and explains the exact bottleneck.
