## Slide 2: The Problem (The Sunday Roster Nightmare)
- **1.2 Million** independent cafes, restaurants, and retail stores in North America & Europe schedule staff manually every week.
- **6.5 Hours/Week:** Average time spent by general managers collecting leave requests, juggling Excel spreadsheets, and resolving scheduling conflicts.
- **Overtime Bleed:** Unintentional labor law violations and overtime costs average **$620/month per location**.
- **The Call-Out Chaos:** When an employee calls out sick 2 hours before a shift, managers panic-text 10 people or end up working the grill themselves.

---

## Slide 3: The Solution (AutoRoster AI MVP)
A streamlined 4-step workflow designed for zero friction:
1. **Screen 1 — Staff & Skills:** Pre-loaded seed profiles with skill tags (Barista, Cook, Supervisor) and weekly hour caps.
2. **Screen 2 — AI Leave Ingestion:** Zero-shot NLP converts messy texts (*"sister's getting married Friday night"*) into structured requests with 1-click manager approval.
3. **Screen 3 — Hard-Constraint Solver:** A millisecond-fast Constraint Satisfaction Problem (CSP) solver that strictly enforces coverage, approved leaves, skills, weekly caps, and max 1 shift/day.
4. **Screen 4 — Ranked Emergency Replacement:** When someone calls out, filters ineligible staff with clear explanations and ranks eligible replacements by remaining headroom and burnout score.

## Slide 5: The 5 Non-Negotiable Hard Constraints
1. **Shift Coverage:** Every required role for every shift is filled.
2. **Approved Leave:** Any employee with approved leave is strictly excluded.
3. **Qualified Skills:** Employees only assigned to roles they are certified for.
4. **Weekly Hour Limit:** No worker exceeds their specified weekly maximum (e.g. 32h/40h).
5. **Max One Shift Per Day:** No double-booking on the same calendar day.
